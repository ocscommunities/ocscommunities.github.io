    How it works

    This is a simple directory of parent-run groups at Ocean Charter School. Families use it to find each other: by grade, interest, language, or shared experience.

    Joining a group

    Click Join. You'll go to the group's WhatsApp, Facebook, or other page. The person who started the group lets new members in from there.

    Starting a group

    Create your group in the app you like, then add it here. It shows up right away. Your email stays private. It's only used if we need to reach you about your group.

    Broken link?

    Click Report broken link under the group, and we'll look into it.

    Change or remove a group

    Email hello@example.com from the address you used when you added it.
